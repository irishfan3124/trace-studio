WELCOME TO YOUR STUDIO

From inspiration
to your own creation.

  1. Set up your surface. Secure your phone on a stand above your paper, with the rear camera facing down.
  2. Choose your inspiration. Choose a drawing from the library or upload your own image. For uploads, use Outline for extracted edges or Original for the full picture.
  3. Line it up. Enable the camera, drag and pinch the artwork, and adjust opacity, size, and rotation.
  4. Lock, look, and trace. Lock the overlay and draw while watching your phone screen. Keep the phone and paper still.

This is a screen overlay, not a light projector or world-anchored AR. Moving the phone changes alignment. Camera access requires permission and a secure connection.

Open in Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android. You can add the page to your home screen from your browser menu.
