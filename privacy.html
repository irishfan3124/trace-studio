Privacy

Trace Studio processes your camera view and artwork in your browser.

Camera and images

When you choose Enable camera, your browser may ask for permission. The camera feed is displayed on your device. Photos you select and outlines made from them stay in the browser. Trace Studio does not send them to a server or record the feed.

Use Turn camera off or leave the workspace to stop the feed. You can change camera permission in your browser’s site settings.

Website requests

When you visit, the hosting provider receives the requests needed to deliver the pages and may log information such as your IP address. The site also requests typefaces from Google Fonts. Trace Studio currently has no accounts, analytics scripts, or advertising scripts.

Questions

Report a privacy question or issue through the project’s issue tracker. Issues are public, so leave out personal details and private images.
