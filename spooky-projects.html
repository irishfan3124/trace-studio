Three spooky drawing projects

Open Browse drawings and choose Spooky & Horror for the outlines used below.

Make a floating ghost postcard

Pick Sheet ghost and drag it slightly above the center of the card. Trace the face lightly, then draw the sheet in long strokes from the head down to the hem. Leave white space around the edges and add a few hand-drawn stars. Draw a short, soft oval beneath the ghost so it looks like it’s floating.

Design a jack-o’-lantern card

Trace the pumpkin silhouette and stem first. Give the eyes and mouth a little extra space before adding the grooves. Color the pumpkin with two oranges, using the darker one between the grooves. A pale yellow in the mouth and eyes gives it a warm glow. Leave room below the pumpkin for a name or greeting.

Compose a haunted mansion scene

Choose Haunted mansion and fit the house in the lower half of the page. Trace its roofline and walls before the windows. Then switch to Vampire bat, shrink the overlay, and place it in the empty sky. Make a second bat smaller and farther from the house. Varying their sizes makes the scene feel deeper.

When combining drawings, trace each one in pencil before you switch overlays. Keep the paper in place as you resize and position the next drawing.
